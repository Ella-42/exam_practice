Exercise 01 – Polymorphic Power

Files to turn in: Warlock.hpp, Warlock.cpp, main.cpp + previous files
Authorized libraries: <string>, <iostream>

🧙‍♂️ Summary:

Your wizard was lonely. It’s time to introduce a Warlock class that inherits from Wizard. Warlocks are Wizards, but they introduce themselves differently and have unique powers (for now, just one: shouting).

🛠 Instructions:

    Create a Warlock class that inherits publicly from Wizard.

    Override the introduce() method to this format:

        <name>: *grins* They call me <name>, <title>. Fear me.

Add a method:

void shoutSpell(void) const;

Output:

    <name>: 🔥 *casts Fireball* 🔥

🧪 Example:

int main(void)
{
    Warlock tom("Tom", "the cursed one");
    tom.introduce();
    tom.shoutSpell();
}

Output:

Tom: *grins* They call me Tom, the cursed one. Fear me.
Tom: 🔥 *casts Fireball* 🔥
